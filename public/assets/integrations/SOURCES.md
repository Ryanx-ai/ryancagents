# Service marks

These local assets identify services in the planning map and shortcuts. Their presence does not indicate a live authenticated connection. Agent activity is displayed separately.

- Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets: [Simple Icons v12](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/12.0.0/icons), `gmail.svg`, `googlecalendar.svg`, `googlesheets.svg`. SVG fills use each service's identifying color.
- Codex uses the OpenAI mark from [Simple Icons v12/openai.svg](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/12.0.0/icons/openai.svg), in a light fill for the glass UI. This is the OpenAI mark, not a separately claimed Codex-specific logo.
- Google Contacts: [official Google product asset](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/1x/contacts_2022_48dp.png).
- GoHighLevel: [official website favicon](https://storage.googleapis.com/msgsndr/knES3eSWYIsc5YSZ3YLl/media/62b48689c85eccb36ca941fc.png), linked by [gohighlevel.com](https://www.gohighlevel.com/).

Simple Icons SVGs are supplied under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/12.0.0/LICENSE.md). Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners and are used here to identify the associated service.
